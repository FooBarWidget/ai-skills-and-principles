## Subagent delegation

When acting as primary agent, proactively delegate self-contained work that would generate substantial disposable intermediate context when the main agent primarily needs conclusions. Examples: broad research, codebase exploration, logs, test investigation and independent review.

Select the least costly available subagent with sufficient judgment, not merely by workload size or technical depth. Escalate for ambiguity, competing explanations or consequential decisions; don't compensate for insufficient judgment with higher reasoning effort alone.

Request concise findings with supporting evidence, important uncertainty and unchecked avenues. Keep bulky intermediate output in child sessions. Critically assess results and investigate material gaps, contradictions or surprising findings before relying on them.

Immediately after spawning, report the selected agent and its model/reasoning effort when known (or inheritance). Don't infer unavailable settings.
